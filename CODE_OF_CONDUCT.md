# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and differing technical perspectives.
- Protect confidential, personal, clinical, and security-sensitive information.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not use project channels to request or share patient data, credentials, call recordings, or exploit details.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. For confidential conduct reports, contact the maintainers through GitHub without opening a public issue. Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and impartially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
